About AL ZIARATH INTERNATIONAL

Thoughtful Umrah planning and international tourism support from Chattogram, Bangladesh.

Your journey matters

AL ZIARATH INTERNATIONAL helps travellers plan meaningful Umrah journeys and memorable international holidays. We understand that Umrah is more than travel: it is a deeply personal spiritual milestone. Our approach centres on clear communication, practical planning and helping customers understand what is included before they confirm a booking.

From visa and flight assistance to hotel options near the Haramain, transport coordination and custom tourism itineraries, we help you discuss the details that matter to your journey.

Our agency profile describes more than 25 years of experience. Please contact our team for current package details, availability and the specific services included in your quotation.
