Compare the details, not only the headline price.

Start by comparing the total duration and the number of nights in Makkah and Madinah. Ask how transfers between cities and hotels are arranged.

Check the exact hotel names, location and room-sharing basis in writing. A hotel category or a general area alone may not tell you how convenient the stay will be.

Confirm flight options, baggage allowance, meals, ground transport, visa arrangements and assistance during the trip. Ask which items may create additional costs.

Request a clear payment schedule, cancellation policy and written receipt for every payment. Keep all confirmed documents together for easy reference.

The best package is the one whose confirmed inclusions match your needs and budget. Ask questions before booking if any detail is unclear.
