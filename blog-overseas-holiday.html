A simple planning framework for international trips.

Choose your destination and approximate travel dates first. Consider weather, trip length, the interests of your group and the time needed for transfers.

Check passport, visa and entry requirements using current official sources for your destination. Requirements depend on nationality, travel purpose and transit route.

Compare accommodation location as well as price. Think about transport access, cancellation terms, meal options and the needs of everyone travelling.

Keep copies of bookings, insurance information and emergency contacts accessible. Share your itinerary with someone you trust and know how to contact your accommodation or travel provider.

Before paying, request a written itinerary that explains what is included, what remains to be paid and the terms for changing or cancelling arrangements.
